How it is calculated

  • EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1), where r is the monthly rate and n the number of months.
  • Interest for a day = (outstanding − savings balance) × rate ÷ 365, never below zero. Example: ₹32,00,000 at 7.30% with nothing in savings is ₹640 a day; with ₹5,00,000 in the linked account it is ₹540.
  • The EMI stays the same. Because less of it goes to interest, more repays the loan, so it closes sooner.
  • This page works month by month with the balance you enter. Real balances move every day; to follow your actual loan day by day, with your bank's own figures, use the tracker (or look at the demo).
