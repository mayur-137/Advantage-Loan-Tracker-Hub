Privacy

Advantage Loan Tracker · last updated 7 October 2026 (ads added)

What is stored

  • Your Google account's name, email address and photo, used to sign you in.
  • For each loan you add: its name and bank, the loan terms (amount, rate, tenure, dates), disbursements, rate changes, extra payments, savings balances, statement entries (date, amount, description, balance), and anything you enter in Smart payoff, Tax and Spending, such as salary and expenses.
  • If you set a salary password, only a salted hash of it is stored, never the password itself.
  • Daily backups of each loan's data, kept with the loan.

Bank statements

Statement files (PDF, Excel, CSV) are read in your browser. The files themselves are never uploaded. Only the dates, amounts, descriptions and balances in them are saved.

Who can see your data

  • You, the owner of the loan.
  • People you approve for that loan. View-only people never see your salary, Smart payoff, Tax or Spending. People you make an editor can see and change everything except backups and the sharing list.
  • The app's administrator can open any loan, with all of its data including salary, read-only, to give support and keep the service working. The administrator cannot change or delete your data, and the database rules enforce this.

Your loan data is never sold, never shared with advertisers and not used for anything else.

Ads and cookies

  • The public pages (home, calculator, articles, guide, demo) and, on wide screens, the space beside the tracker show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technology to show ads and measure them, based on your visits to this and other sites. See how Google uses data from partner sites.
  • You can turn off personalised ads at adssettings.google.com (or aboutads.info).
  • Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.
  • The ad code runs in the page like any other script. The tracker never sends your loan figures, salary or statements to it, and no ads are shown on the sign-in, terms and privacy screens.
  • The tracker itself keeps a copy of your data in your browser (for offline use) and remembers small settings such as the last tab you opened.

Where it is kept

In Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Mumbai region asia-south1), protected by Google sign-in and database security rules.

Deleting your data

Open the loan, then choose Delete this loan from the account menu (your photo, top right) or at the end of Loan settings. This deletes the loan and everything in it, backups included, for good. Download a backup file first if you may want the data later.

Not financial advice

Figures are estimates based on what you enter. Your bank's statement and interest certificate are the final word.
