What it tells you

Your payoff date

When the loan closes, and how a bigger balance, a prepayment or a rate change moves it.

Bank match

Checks the EMI that left your savings reached the loan, and the interest and outstanding agree to the rupee.

Smart payoff

Salary, expenses and card-bill timing turned into a payoff plan, and the monthly amount for a debt-free goal.

Tax

Sec 24(b) interest and Sec 80C principal by financial year, old vs new regime.

Who it is for

Anyone with a home loan linked to a savings or current account (an overdraft-style loan), from any bank, where interest is charged only on the outstanding loan minus the balance in that account. It works for an ordinary home loan too: just leave the savings balance at zero. How these loans work

Start in four steps

  1. Sign in with Google: no new password to remember.
  2. Add your loan from your sanction letter: amount, rate, tenure, disbursal and first EMI date.
  3. Upload your statements (PDF, Excel or CSV from your bank's website or app) or type the balance.
  4. Check Bank match after each EMI. Share the loan with family as a Viewer or Editor if you like.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The site shows ads from Google to pay for itself; the tracker has no paid plan.

Which banks does it work with?

Any bank. Statements are read by their column names (date, debit, credit, balance); if a layout isn't recognised you pick the columns once and it is remembered.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is a calculator for your own figures. Your bank's statement and interest certificate are the final word.
