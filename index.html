    Salary password

    Show salary needs a password: if none is set yet you set one first, then the salary unlocks; after that it asks for it each time. After you unlock, the salary stays visible until you close the tab, tap Hide, or leave the page idle for 5 minutes. It hides what is on screen; people signed in as owner or admin can still read the salary in the database.

    Your monthly routine

      Rules that keep it working

      • Leave the surplus in the linked savings account. Money that leaves stops saving interest.
      • Skip FDs and keep the emergency fund here. Every rupee saves 7.30% loan interest, tax free; an FD pays less and is taxed.
      • Pay the full credit card bill on the due date, every time. Card interest is over 36% a year and wipes out the gain.
      • Put bonuses, incentives and refunds straight into this account.
      • Money must be in the account at the end of the day. Money that comes in and goes out the same day saves nothing.
      • When RBI cuts the repo rate, check that your rate falls too. If it doesn't, ask the bank to reset the spread.

      Loan details

      Projection

      Salary, expenses and how you pay them are set in the Smart payoff tab. After your last balance entry the tracker uses them to estimate the savings balance day by day, with the EMI taken out on each EMI date.

      Loan amount is the sanctioned amount; what the bank has actually paid out goes under Disbursements below. Leave EMI empty to use the standard formula. If your bank EMI is different, type the exact amount. Interest is worked out daily on a 365-day year and charged with each EMI.

      Daily backups

      A copy of everything is saved in your Firebase database every day: when you open the tracker, and by a daily job in GitHub's cloud at 11:30 PM. If a new copy is exactly the same as the one before it, the older copy is removed; otherwise backups are never changed or deleted. Restoring adds that backup's entries back and replaces entries with the same date.

      Delete this loan

      Deletes this loan and everything in it for good: loan settings, salary plan, balances, statements, rates, extra payments, tax and spending settings, the salary password, all backups and the sharing list. People it is shared with lose access straight away. This can't be undone. Download a backup file first if you may want the data later.
